# General important news profile

Use this profile for consequential current events in China and internationally.

Typical topics include major public policy changes, diplomacy, international relations,
elections, armed conflicts, major economic developments, energy, public safety,
natural disasters, significant regulatory actions, and major social events.

Prefer events with broad public, economic, institutional, or international impact.

Do not prioritize entertainment gossip, sports results, minor local incidents,
routine corporate promotion, clickbait, or trivial updates.
