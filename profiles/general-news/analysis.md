# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how important this news is for a reader who wants to understand major
developments in China and the world without being overwhelmed by low-value news.

# Scoring rubric

- 9-10: Major event with national, international, systemic, economic, security,
  or large-scale human impact.
- 7-8: Important development worth knowing today, with meaningful effects on
  policy, society, markets, technology, international relations, or public life.
- 5-6: Useful context or moderate development, but not essential today.
- 3-4: Routine news, narrow impact, repetitive coverage, or weak new information.
- 0-2: Gossip, clickbait, promotional content, speculation, or trivial events.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider:
- scale of impact
- number of people affected
- economic or policy significance
- international significance
- urgency
- novelty
- reliability of the source
- whether the event changes the reader's understanding of an important issue

Do not reward sensational headlines.

Distinguish confirmed facts from allegations, predictions, political claims,
company claims, and commentary.

Do not assign higher scores because an article supports a particular political
or ideological position.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
